Why attend?

Adventure, competition and Mozambican hospitality.

Mozambique Challenge 2026 is built to blend rugged landscapes, thrilling terrain and the raw beauty of Mozambique’s coastline.

The rally is designed for experienced drivers, family crews, novice routes, sponsor activations and a social event atmosphere at Blue Ocean Residential.

Program

Race week outline.

02 Oct · 08:00

Welcome seafood BBQ

Blue Ocean Residential hosts the rally kick-off. The welcome BBQ is included in the entry.

03 Oct · 09:00

Round 1

Timed start every five minutes from the beach, followed by 56 km of scenic beach, rolling dunes and bush trails. Evening dinner with live music.

04 Oct

Round 2

Tide-dependent 09:00 start for another 56 km route through scenic beach, rolling dunes and bush trails.

05 Oct · 09:00

Round 3

Tide-dependent 56 km route from Blue Ocean Residential, with a beach bonfire planned if weather allows.

06 Oct

Round 4 and farewell party

Tide-dependent 56 km route, lamb spit feast at Blue Ocean, prize giving and farewell party with live music.
